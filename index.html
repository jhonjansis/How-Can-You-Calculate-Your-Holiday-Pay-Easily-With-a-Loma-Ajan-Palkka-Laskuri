What Is a Loma-Ajan Palkka Laskuri?

A loma-ajan palkka laskuri is a calculator designed to estimate the money an employee may receive during annual leave. It helps users understand their expected holiday income before taking time off.

In Finland, annual holiday entitlements are mainly governed by the Finnish Annual Holidays Act (Vuosilomalaki). The calculation method can vary depending on whether an employee receives a monthly salary, hourly wages, or another form of compensation.

A holiday pay calculator can help estimate:
  • Holiday pay based on salary.
  • Holiday bonus (lomaraha).
  • Compensation for unused annual leave.
  • Estimated holiday earnings.
  • The financial value of accrued holiday days.

It is particularly helpful for employees who want to check their payslips or plan their holiday budgets.

What Is the Difference Between Lomaraha and Lomapalkka?

Many employees confuse lomaraha and lomapalkka because both are connected to annual holidays. However, they are not exactly the same.

Lomapalkka (Holiday Pay)

Lomapalkka (holiday pay) is the payment an employee receives for their annual leave. It ensures that taking approved holidays does not normally mean losing regular employment income.

Lomaraha (Holiday Bonus)

Lomaraha (holiday bonus) is an additional payment commonly provided under collective agreements or employment contracts. It is not a universal statutory entitlement under the Finnish Annual Holidays Act.

A lomarahalaskuri can help estimate this additional payment when the employee is entitled to receive it.

Example:

Some collective agreements provide a holiday bonus equal to 50% of holiday pay. However, the exact percentage and eligibility conditions depend on the applicable agreement.

How to Calculate Holiday Pay in Finland

The calculation of holiday pay depends on the employee's salary structure and working arrangement.

1. Monthly Salaried Employees

Employees receiving a monthly salary generally receive holiday pay based on their regular salary and the applicable calculation rules.

For example, if an employee earns €3,000 per month, their holiday pay is generally calculated using the relevant monthly salary method and applicable adjustments.

2. Hourly Paid Employees

Hourly paid employees may have their holiday pay calculated using a percentage-based method or another applicable approach.

The calculation can depend on working days, hours worked, earnings during the holiday credit year, and the employee's employment history.

3. Percentage-Based Holiday Pay

For certain employees, holiday pay is calculated as a percentage of eligible earnings.

The applicable percentage may depend on the length of the employment relationship and the relevant statutory provisions.

What Is a Lomakorvaus Laskuri?

A lomakorvaus laskuri helps estimate holiday compensation for unused annual leave.

Holiday compensation is particularly relevant when employment ends and an employee has accumulated holiday days that have not been taken.

For example, if an employee leaves a company before using all their earned annual holidays, they may be entitled to financial compensation for those unused days.

The amount depends on the employee's holiday entitlement, earnings, employment arrangement, and applicable rules.

A holiday compensation calculator can make it easier to understand the estimated value of unused leave before reviewing the final settlement with an employer.

How Many Holiday Days Can You Accumulate in Finland?

Annual holiday entitlement in Finland generally depends on how long the employment relationship has continued by the end of the holiday credit year.

Under the Finnish Annual Holidays Act, employees generally accrue:

  • 2 holiday days for each full holiday credit month when employment has lasted less than one year by the end of the holiday credit year.
  • 2.5 holiday days for each full holiday credit month when employment has lasted at least one year by the end of the holiday credit year.

The holiday credit year runs from 1 April to 31 March.

Other conditions determine whether a month qualifies for holiday accrual. The applicable working-day or working-hours rules should therefore be checked.

A lomalaskuri can help employees estimate their accumulated holiday entitlement and understand how their available days may affect their holiday income.
